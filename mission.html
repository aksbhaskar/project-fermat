Mission

Mathematics should not be a lottery.

Access to meaningful mathematical education should not depend on where a student is born, or what their family can afford.

We want mathematics to be more accessible, engaging and rigorous for students from underserved communities.

Our mission is to connect those students with passionate university students, educators and mentors — people who love the subject and want to pass that on.

We start at Classes 6 to 8, early enough that a student’s sense of the subject is still being formed, and work along the route they actually travel: SOF IMO and NMTC, building toward IOQM and RMO.

The aim is not that every student wins something. It is that they stop believing mathematics is a thing other people are good at.
