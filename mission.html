Mission

Mathematics should not be a lottery.

Access to meaningful mathematical education should not depend on where a student is born, or what their family can afford.

01

What we believe

We want mathematics to be more accessible, engaging and rigorous for students from underserved communities.

Our mission is to connect those students with passionate university students, educators and mentors — people who love the subject and want to pass that on.

Through classroom teaching, olympiad-oriented problem solving, personalised learning and open educational resources, we aim to help students build mathematical confidence, develop independent reasoning, and discover the joy of mathematics.

We are building a community where students can explore mathematics beyond the textbook, and where those who love the subject can help others discover it.

02

Where we start

We begin with Classes 6 to 8 — early enough that a student’s sense of the subject is still being formed.

Olympiad mathematics is usually framed as something for students who are already ahead. We think it works better as an invitation: a way of showing that mathematics is not a list of procedures to memorise but something you can think your way through.

We work along the route students actually travel — SOF IMO and NMTC in the early years, building toward IOQM and RMO — without treating the exams as the point.

The aim is not that every student wins something. It is that they stop believing mathematics is a thing other people are good at.
