Project Fermat

Mission

Access to meaningful mathematical education should not depend on where a student is born, or what their family can afford.

We want mathematics to be more accessible, engaging and rigorous for students from underserved communities, by connecting them with university students, educators and mentors who love the subject and want to pass that on.

Where we start

We begin at Classes 6 to 8, early enough that a student's sense of the subject is still being formed, and work along the route they actually travel — SOF IMO and NMTC, building toward IOQM and RMO.

The aim is not that every student wins something. It is that they stop believing mathematics is a thing other people are good at.

What we are building

A community where students can explore mathematics beyond the textbook, and where those who love the subject can help others discover it.
