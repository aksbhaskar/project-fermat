Founders

Project Fermat is run by the two of us, alongside the volunteer scholars who teach with us.

Ishaan Harish

Founder

Mathematics · Research · Education

Ishaan is a mathematics undergraduate at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, with interests spanning mathematical research, probability and stochastic processes. Having once struggled with mathematics himself, he understands how much a good teacher can change a student’s relationship with the subject.

Akshat Bhaskar

Founder

Education · Research · Community building

Akshat is a student and researcher interested in mathematics, education, public policy, and building initiatives that make knowledge more accessible. He has worked on education and financial-literacy initiatives, taught mathematics, and built student-led organisations.
