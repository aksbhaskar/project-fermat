Founders

Two students who kept teaching anyway.

Project Fermat is run by the two of us, alongside the volunteers who teach with us.

Akshat Bhaskar

Co-founder

Education · Research · Community building

Akshat is a student and researcher interested in mathematics, education, public policy, and building initiatives that make knowledge more accessible.

He has worked on education and financial-literacy initiatives, taught mathematics, conducted research, and built student-led organisations — developing programmes, coordinating volunteers, and creating opportunities for students to engage with ideas beyond their classrooms.

Through this initiative, he hopes to help more students discover the value of mathematical thinking and gain access to learning opportunities they might otherwise miss.

Ishaan Harish

Co-founder

Mathematics · Research · Education

Ishaan is a mathematics undergraduate at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, with interests spanning mathematical research, probability and stochastic processes.

Having once struggled with mathematics himself, he understands how much a good teacher can change a student’s relationship with the subject. Through this initiative, he hopes to help students encounter mathematics earlier, develop confidence in problem solving, and discover its depth beyond the classroom.

He is also involved in mathematical research and technical projects, and is interested in building systems that make learning more accessible.
