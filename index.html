Mathematics beyond the textbook.

A volunteer initiative helping middle school students (Classes 6–8) build a foundation in olympiad mathematics — from SOF IMO and NMTC toward IOQM and RMO.

Less about solving a particular problem, more about learning how to look at one.

What we do

01

Foundations first

Olympiad work rewards students who are secure in the basics. We start there.

02

Problem solving

Sessions built around the habit of sitting with a hard problem, and being wrong without giving up.

03

A pathway, not a cram

We follow the route students actually travel — SOF IMO and NMTC, building toward IOQM and RMO.

04

Open resources

Notes and problem sets released freely, so the work reaches students we cannot teach in person.

Volunteer

If you love thinking about problems — and about how to explain them — you already have what we are looking for.

We’re looking for Class 11–12 students and mathematics undergraduates who want to teach. No formal teaching experience needed.

Reach out with a short introduction and your mathematical background.
