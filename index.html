01

Mission

To make mathematics more accessible, engaging and rigorous for students from underserved communities.

We believe that access to meaningful mathematical education should not depend on where a student is born or what their family can afford.

Our mission is to connect those students with passionate university students, educators and mentors — through classroom teaching, olympiad-oriented problem solving, personalised learning and open educational resources.

We aim to help students build mathematical confidence, develop independent reasoning, and discover the joy of mathematics.

We are building a community where students can explore mathematics beyond the textbook, and where those who love the subject can help others discover it.

02

What we do

01

Classroom teaching

Volunteers teach directly in schools and community classrooms, working alongside teachers to strengthen the foundations students are expected to build on.

02

Olympiad problem solving

Structured problem-solving sessions that introduce students to competition mathematics — and, more importantly, to the habit of sitting with a hard problem.

03

Personalised learning

Small-group and one-to-one mentorship, so that a student who has fallen behind is met where they are rather than moved past.

04

Open educational resources

Notes, problem sets and explanations released freely, so that the work outlasts any single session and reaches students we cannot teach in person.

Founders

Ishaan Harish

Co-founder

Mathematics · Research · Education

Ishaan is a mathematics undergraduate at Sri Venkateswara College, University of Delhi, with interests spanning mathematical research, probability and stochastic processes.

Having once struggled with mathematics himself, he understands how much a good teacher can change a student's relationship with the subject. Through this initiative, he hopes to help students encounter mathematics earlier, develop confidence in problem solving, and discover its depth beyond the classroom.

04

Volunteer

If you love mathematics, you already have the thing we are looking for.

We are recruiting university students, educators and mentors who want to teach. You do not need a teaching qualification — you need patience, a real grasp of the material, and the willingness to show up consistently.

Volunteers are supported with material, structure and a community of people doing the same work.

R1

Classroom volunteer

Teach scheduled sessions in partner schools and community classrooms.

R2

Olympiad mentor

Run problem-solving sessions and guide students preparing for competitions.

R3

Curriculum & resources

Write notes, problem sets and explanations for our open educational resources.

R4

Operations & outreach

Coordinate volunteers, build school partnerships, and keep the programmes running.

Applications are open. Tell us who you are, what you would like to teach, and how much time you can give. We read every application.
