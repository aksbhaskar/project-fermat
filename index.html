01

Mission

Access to meaningful mathematical education should not depend on where a student is born, or what their family can afford.

Our mission is to make mathematics more accessible, engaging and rigorous for students from underserved communities, by connecting them with passionate university students, educators and mentors.

Through classroom teaching, olympiad-oriented problem solving, personalised learning and open educational resources, we aim to help students build mathematical confidence, develop independent reasoning, and discover the joy of mathematics.

We are building a community where students can explore mathematics beyond the textbook, and where those who love the subject can help others discover it.

02

What we do

01

Foundations first

Olympiad work rewards students who are secure in the basics. We start there — closing the gaps that make everything afterwards feel impossible.

02

Problem solving

Structured sessions built around the habit of sitting with a hard problem: reading it properly, trying something, and being wrong without giving up.

03

A pathway, not a cram

We work along the route students actually travel — SOF IMO and NMTC in the early years, building toward IOQM and RMO.

04

Open resources

Notes, problem sets and worked explanations released freely, so the work outlasts any single session and reaches students we cannot teach in person.

04

Volunteer

If you love thinking about problems — and about how to explain them — you already have what we are looking for.

We're looking for Class 11–12 students and mathematics undergraduates who want to teach. No formal teaching experience needed.

What matters is patience, a real grasp of the material, and the willingness to show up consistently. Volunteers are supported with material, structure, and a community of people doing the same work.

R1

Tutor

Teach scheduled sessions to small groups of Class 6–8 students.

R2

Problem-set author

Write and curate problems, worked solutions and notes for our open resources.

R3

Mentor

Work one-to-one with students preparing for IOQM and RMO.

R4

Operations & outreach

Coordinate volunteers, build school partnerships, and keep the programmes running.

Applications are open

Reach out with a short introduction and your mathematical background. Tell us what you would like to teach and how much time you can give — we read every email.
