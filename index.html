Project Fermat

A small volunteer initiative helping middle school students (Classes 6–8) in underserved schools across Delhi NCR build a foundation in olympiad mathematics — from SOF IMO and NMTC toward IOQM and RMO. Less about solving a particular problem, more about learning how to look at one.

What we do

  • Foundations first

    Olympiad work rewards students who are secure in the basics. We start there.

  • Problem solving

    Sessions built around the habit of sitting with a hard problem, and being wrong without giving up.

  • A pathway, not a cram

    We follow the route students actually travel, rather than treating the exams as the point.

  • Open resources

    Notes and problem sets released freely, so the work reaches students we cannot teach in person.

Volunteer as a tutor

We're looking for undergraduates, senior school students, and teachers who are genuinely good at mathematics and want to teach it — people who'd rather spend a Saturday on a hard problem than almost anything else.
