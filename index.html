Est. 2026 · Delhi NCR

Whether a child finds out they’re good at mathematics still depends too much on the school they land in. The ability is spread evenly. The teaching isn’t.

We exist to close that gap, one student at a time.

What we do

A real mathematical education, not exam drilling.

We teach the way we wish we’d been taught in sixth grade — starting from the basics and building the habit of thinking about problems.

Foundations

Secure in the basics

Olympiad work rewards students who own the fundamentals. We start there, patiently, before anything else.

Method

Problem-solving, not procedures

Sessions built around sitting with a hard problem — and being wrong for a while without giving up.

Pathway

The route they’ll actually travel

We follow the real competition ladder, so students meet it years before most of their peers hear it exists.

Access

Open resources

Notes and problem sets released freely, so the work reaches students we can’t yet teach in person.

The pathway

From the basics to the national olympiads.

Most students never hear this pathway exists until Class 10 or 11 — by which point they’ve lost years they can’t get back. We start it early.
